Privacy Policy

App: Zen Mahjong Solitaire (com.zenmahjong.game) • Effective Date: November 16, 2026
Key Summary: Zen Mahjong Solitaire is a relaxing single-player puzzle game. All player progress, scores, and preferences remain 100% on your device. The app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Process

When you play Zen Mahjong Solitaire, we respect your privacy and follow strict data safety principles:

  • No Account Required: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social logins.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We never collect sensitive personal information, biometric data, or precise GPS locations.
  • Local Gameplay Storage: All game data (levels completed, star ratings, best times, player customization, and settings) is stored exclusively on your device's private sandboxed storage (user://zen_mahjong_save.json) and is never transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and keep the game free to play, the App uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Google AdMob may process certain pseudonymous technical data to deliver, measure, and protect advertisements:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and IP address (used to infer coarse country/city level location).
  • Performance & Analytics: Ad impression data, click metrics, and diagnostic error reports.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Advertising Technologies

3. Managing Your Ad Preferences (Opting Out)

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device settings:

Settings > Google (or Privacy) > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID)

4. Permissions

The App requests only technical permissions necessary for gameplay and ad display:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load ads from Google AdMob servers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Checks whether network connectivity is available before requesting ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Android Advertising ID permission used by Google AdMob for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

The App does NOT request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed for general audiences and casual puzzle players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). AdMob requests comply with applicable child protection guidelines.

6. Data Deletion

All game data is stored exclusively on your device. To erase all saved game data, you can clear the app's cache and data through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Zen Mahjong > Storage > Clear Storage), or simply uninstall the application.
