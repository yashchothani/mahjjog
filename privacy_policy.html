Privacy Policy

App: Zen Mahjong Solitaire (com.zenmahjong.game) • Effective Date: October 29, 2026
Key Summary: Zen Mahjong Solitaire is an offline single-player puzzle game. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers.

1. Information Collection & Use

Zen Mahjong Solitaire is designed with complete privacy in mind. When you play our game:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or social media accounts.
  • No Tracking & Analytics: We do not integrate tracking tools (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or third-party telemetry).
  • No Advertising: There are zero banner ads, interstitials, rewarded videos, or advertising identifier (GAID) trackers.

2. Local Device Storage

All gameplay information is saved exclusively on your device's private sandboxed storage (user://zen_mahjong_save.json):

  • Level progression, star ratings, and personal best completion times
  • Player customization preferences (nickname, avatar icon, and border frame)
  • Audio levels (music and sound effects volume) and accessibility toggles
  • Daily streak counter and login reward progress

This data never leaves your device and is never synchronized to external servers.

3. Permissions

Zen Mahjong Solitaire does not request access to sensitive Android permissions such as your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or external files.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is fully compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, the game is safe for players of all ages.

5. Data Deletion

You have complete control over your local data. To erase all saved game data, you can clear the app's cache and data through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Zen Mahjong > Storage > Clear Storage), or simply uninstall the application.
