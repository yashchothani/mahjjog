## Original Game Artwork & Audio
- **Visual Assets:**
  - `assets/backgrounds/menu_bg.jpg`: Original digital painting generated specifically for Zen Mahjong (tranquil Japanese Zen garden with morning mist and raked sand).
  - `assets/backgrounds/gameplay_bg.jpg`: Original textured deep jade stone surface with bamboo silhouette border.
  - `assets/icons/app_icon.jpg`: Original ivory ceramic Green Dragon Mahjong tile icon.
- **Audio Assets:**
  - Procedurally generated 16-bit PCM acoustic waveforms synthesized via GDScript (`scripts/audio/audio_manager.gd`). Free from external copyright claims or royalties.
